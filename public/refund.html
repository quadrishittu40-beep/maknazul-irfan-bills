Refund and Failed-Delivery Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Please confirm: the time limits below are the ones Maknazul 'Irfan Academy commits to. Adjust them before you go live if your processes differ.

If you paid but did not get the service

  1. Wait 30 minutes, because some deliveries are delayed by networks or providers.
  2. Contact us on 08084929862 (call or WhatsApp) or at maknazulirfanacademy@gmail.com with your payment reference and the email used.
  3. We will check the payment and the delivery status and either complete the delivery or refund you.

When we refund

  • Your payment was successful but the service could not be delivered.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Bank processing can take several working days after we approve the refund.

When we cannot refund

  • The service was delivered correctly, for example airtime sent to the number you entered or a PIN already issued.
  • You entered a wrong phone number, meter number, smart card number or other detail.
  • The service has been used or redeemed, such as an examination PIN that has been used.

Report a problem

Please contact us as soon as possible and within 7 days of payment. Include your payment reference.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Maknazul 'Irfan Academy respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect, why, and who we share it with.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address from Google when you sign in.
  • Order details: your name, email, the phone number, meter number or reference you enter, and the service you choose.
  • Payment records: payment reference, amount, service and status. Card and bank details are entered only on Paystack's secure page, and we do not see or store them.
  • On your device: a list of your recent transactions is saved in your own browser storage.

Why we use it

  • To create your account and let you sign in
  • To process your payment and deliver your service
  • To give you support and handle refunds
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure

Who we share it with

Only as needed to provide the service: Paystack (payments), Google and Firebase (sign-in), our hosting provider, and the service providers that deliver airtime, data, electricity, TV or examination services. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep records for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes and meet legal or accounting requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal information, subject to legal record-keeping needs. We aim to handle personal data in line with applicable Nigerian data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. You can clear the history saved on your device by clearing your browser's site data.
